## English

### Fading Furigana — Support

Fading Furigana is a Safari extension and Mac companion app that adds furigana to Japanese words on web pages and helps you save and review them. Your learning data stays on your Mac.

#### Install & enable the Safari extension

1. Install **Fading Furigana** from the Mac App Store and open the app once.
2. Open **Safari → Settings → Extensions** (or **Safari → Settings for This Website**).
3. Turn on **Fading Furigana**.
4. When Safari asks for website access, allow it. The extension needs page access to find Japanese text and add furigana.
5. Open any Japanese web page — annotations appear automatically.

#### Using it

- Click an annotated word to see its reading and meaning, and to **save** it.
- Mark words as **Known**, **Forgot**, **Ignore**, or **Pin**.
- Open the Mac app to see **Today**, your **Library**, **Discover**, and **Review**.
- Words you save in Safari appear in the Mac app on the same device automatically.

#### Trial & purchase

- New users get a **one-month trial** of local Basic features.
- After the trial, **Basic** is a one-time purchase for local single-platform use (Safari + Mac app on one Mac).
- Your saved words are never deleted when the trial ends or purchase status changes.
- Use **Restore Purchases** in Settings if you reinstall or change Macs.

#### Privacy

Fading Furigana is local-first. No account is required for Basic, and your learning data is stored on your Mac. See the Privacy Policy for details. You can control page-URL retention (off / domain only / full URL) in Settings.

#### Troubleshooting

- **No annotations?** Confirm the extension is enabled in Safari and has access to the current website; reload the page.
- **Saved word missing in the Mac app?** Reopen or refresh the Mac app; check Storage health in Settings (should report shared/native storage, not fallback).
- **Reset local data:** Settings includes storage health and a local reset. Resetting clears local learning data on this Mac.

## 中文

### Fading Furigana —— 支持

Fading Furigana 是一个 Safari 扩展 + Mac 配套 app，会在日语网页的词语上添加假名注音，并帮你保存和复习。学习数据仅保存在你的 Mac 上。

#### 安装并启用 Safari 扩展

1. 从 Mac App Store 安装 **Fading Furigana**，先打开一次 app。
2. 打开 **Safari → 设置 → 扩展**（或 **Safari → 用于此网站的设置**）。
3. 启用 **Fading Furigana**。
4. Safari 询问网站访问权限时请允许。扩展需要页面访问权限来识别日语文本并添加注音。
5. 打开任意日语网页 —— 注音会自动出现。

#### 如何使用

- 点击带注音的词，查看读音和释义，并**保存**。
- 把词标记为**已掌握 / 遗忘 / 忽略 / 置顶**。
- 打开 Mac app 查看 **Today、词库（Library）、Discover、复习**。
- 在 Safari 保存的词会自动出现在同一台 Mac 的 app 中。

#### 试用与购买

- 新用户享有本地 Basic 功能的**一个月试用**。
- 试用结束后，**Basic** 为一次性购买，用于本地单平台使用（一台 Mac 上的 Safari + Mac app）。
- 试用到期或购买状态变化时，已保存的词不会被删除。
- 重装或换 Mac 时，在「设置」中使用**恢复购买**。

#### 隐私

Fading Furigana 本地优先。Basic 不需要账号，学习数据保存在你的 Mac 上。详见隐私政策。你可以在「设置」中控制页面 URL 留存（关闭 / 仅域名 / 完整 URL）。

#### 常见问题

- **没有注音？** 确认扩展已在 Safari 启用并对当前网站有访问权限，然后刷新页面。
- **Mac app 里找不到保存的词？** 重新打开或刷新 Mac app；在「设置」查看存储健康（应显示 shared/native 存储，而非 fallback）。
- **重置本地数据：** 「设置」中含存储健康与本地重置。重置会清除本机的本地学习数据。
