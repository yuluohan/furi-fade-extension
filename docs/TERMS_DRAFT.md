# Terms of Use Draft

Last updated: 2026-06-14

This is draft product copy for the Fading Furigana MVP. Review before publishing and replace the contact/support placeholders.

## Overview

Fading Furigana helps Japanese learners read web pages by adding reading aids, saving vocabulary, and scheduling local review.

The Basic MVP is a local-first macOS app bundled with a Safari Web Extension. It does not require an account.

## Beta Status

Early beta versions may contain bugs, dictionary mistakes, tokenizer mistakes, UI issues, data display errors, or purchase-state testing issues.

Do not rely on beta versions as your only record of important study history. Before resetting or reinstalling the app during beta testing, follow the local recovery guidance in `docs/LOCAL_RECOVERY_GUIDE.md`.

## Purchases

Fading Furigana Basic is planned as a one-time purchase for the local single-platform learning loop after a one-month trial.

Purchases and restore operations use Apple's StoreKit and App Store systems. App Store purchase terms, refunds, taxes, and payment processing are handled by Apple.

Your local learning data should remain present when a trial ends, when purchase status changes, or when purchase verification is temporarily unavailable.

## Accounts And Sync

The Basic MVP does not require an account and does not include cross-device sync.

A future Pro sync feature may require an account and a separate subscription. Future sync terms should be added before that feature is released.

## Local Data

For the Basic MVP, learning data is stored locally on your Mac in the app group container shared by the Mac app and Safari extension.

Deleting the app, resetting local data, manually removing app group files, or using system cleanup tools may remove local learning data.

## Acceptable Use

Do not use Fading Furigana to violate website terms, bypass access controls, scrape protected content, or perform unlawful activity.

The extension is intended to annotate pages that you choose to read in Safari.

## Dictionary And Learning Content

Dictionary meanings, readings, difficulty labels, JLPT-like levels, and learning suggestions may be incomplete or wrong. They are learning aids, not authoritative language instruction.

## Warranty Disclaimer

Fading Furigana is provided as-is for the MVP beta. To the maximum extent allowed by law, no warranty is provided that the app will be uninterrupted, error-free, or suitable for every learning need.

## Limitation Of Liability

To the maximum extent allowed by law, Fading Furigana's liability is limited to the amount you paid for the app or service.
